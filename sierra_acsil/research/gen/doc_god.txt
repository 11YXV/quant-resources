/*============================================================================
  GapOpeningDrive_NQ_MNQ  v3  --  engine + orders + hardening
  (Lessons #12: the two lines above stay at the very top of the file.)

  WHAT IT DOES
    Opening drive on MNQ. When the day session opens away from yesterday's
    close, the overnight move is tested in the first minutes: if the first
    5-minute range breaks on the SAME side as the gap, the overnight buyers
    (sellers) are still in control -> trade with it, tight stop, hold to the
    close. If the other side breaks first, there is no trade that day.

  SOURCE OF THE IDEA
    Kaufman, Trading Systems and Methods 5th ed., ch.16 "Opening Range
    Breakout" (Crabel; volatility-based breakout distances) and ch.15
    "Opening Gaps" (trade in the direction of the gap). Zarattini & Aziz
    (2023), "Can Day Trading Really Be Profitable?" (5-minute opening range,
    stop = 10% of the 14-day ATR, hold to the close). The gap-side filter is
    my own addition, found on 2017-2021 and confirmed on 2022-2024: breaks
    WITH the gap made PF 1.38, breaks AGAINST it PF 1.00.

  RULES (gap up -> long; gap down -> short, the mirror)
    R1 Session = 09:30-16:00 New York. gap = open - prior day-session close.
       avg range = mean high-low of the last 14 full day sessions.
       Holiday-shortened days: no trade.
    R2 Trade only if |gap| >= 0.05 x avg range.
    R3 Opening range = the first 5-minute bar. At its close: a resting STOP
       entry one tick above its high (gap up) / below its low (gap down).
    R4 The order is cancelled when price trades one tick through the OTHER
       side of the range first, or at 10:30 if not filled.
    R5 Attached stop 0.10 x avg range from the fill. No target.
    R6 One trade a day. Flat at 15:55.

  MEASURED (this file in the mock, NQ 1-minute data 2017-01 .. 2024-03, 1 MNQ,
  $2.50 per round trip)
    785 trades | 18.1% winners | profit factor 1.49 | net +$10,740 |

    Chosen on 2017-2021 ($94/month), checked on 2022-2024 ($189/month).
    THE FILL ASSUMPTION MATTERS: when the entry and the stop are both inside
    one 1-minute bar the order of events is unknown. Assuming the stop is hit
    every time, the net is $7,825 (PF 1.35); the mock's tick path gives
    $10,740. Only a tick Replay settles it (lessons #54).
    THE ON-CHART TALLY UNDERSTATES THIS SCRIPT: on 5-minute bars it counts a
    stop-out whenever the entry bar's range contains the stop (PF about 1.06
    on the same data). Judge this script by tick Replay, not by the tally.
    Positive in both periods for ranges of 3-30 minutes, stops 0.05-0.25,
    min gap 0-0.4. A profit target of any size made it worse: the edge is the
    few days that run all day (18% winners, 6.8 : 1 win / loss).
    NOT TESTED: anything after 2024-03-07.

  ORDER LAYER (identical in the three scripts of this suite)
    O1  One trade at a time, one order: quantity N, MaximumPositionAllowed = N.
    O2  The entry carries its own ATTACHED stop (Stop1Offset = a distance from
        the actual fill, so slippage never changes the risk). No target.
    O3  Entry on the first live call after the signal bar closes, at most 120 s
        late (input). Every engine signal is logged "TRADED" or
        "NOT traded -- <reason>".
    O4  Size: fixed contracts, or risk % of the ACCOUNT SIZE INPUT (never the
        broker balance, lessons #5) / the stop distance, capped by Max
        contracts; optional max $ risk per trade (reduces, then skips).
    O5  Risk halts, each OFF at 0 (#36): daily loss incl. the open trade
        (flattens), daily profit lock, losing trades in a row, trades per day.
        Reset only on a new trading day (#43).
    O6  Flat time 15:55 New York: resting entries cancelled, position closed
        with FlattenAndCancelAllOrders(); re-issued after 30 s, MANUAL ACTION
        line after 10 tries (#69). CME holiday / early-close sessions: no
        entries, flat the same distance before the halt (#66).
    O7  Reconciliation on every call from ONE position read (#17): closed
        trades classified from the CumulativeProfitLoss / TotalTrades delta
        (#34) before the daily rollover (#35); FILL VERIFY once per fill
        (#60); every contract covered by a working stop or flatten after 5 s
        (#68); orphan and inherited positions (#38, #70); working orders
        while flat are cancelled (#41).
    O8  Full recalculation: trade state survives, the engine is rebuilt, the
        first live call re-adopts or discards (#37, #38, #63).
    O9  Trade-behaviour settings are written on every call (#15, #16).
    O10 Live orders need 'Allow LIVE orders' = Yes. CHART CHECK (New York time
        zone, 5-minute bars, MNQ/NQ symbol, enough sessions) and the feed
        checks (connected; not delayed for live, #65) block entries.

  THREE BUILD STEPS IN ONE FILE
    Step 1 (engine): 'Orders' = Signals only -> signals, lines and the TALLY,
    no orders. Step 2 (orders) and Step 3 (hardening): 'Orders' = Send orders.
