/*============================================================================
  TrendDayVwapPullback_NQ_MNQ  v3  --  engine + orders + hardening
  (Lessons #12: the two lines above stay at the very top of the file.)

  WHAT IT DOES
    Buys the first pullback on a trend day. Once a 30-minute close is outside
    the noise band (see R2) the day has shown a direction. The script then
    waits for price to come back to session VWAP and be rejected there within
    one 15-minute block, enters with the trend, and holds to the close with a
    stop 0.15 x the average day range away.

  SOURCE OF THE IDEA
    Kaufman, Trading Systems and Methods 5th ed., ch.16: "For directional
    entries, waiting for a pullback will improve entries"; VWAP as the
    intraday fair price (Zarattini & Aziz 2023, "VWAP: the Holy Grail for Day
    Trading Systems"); the noise band of Zarattini, Aziz, Barbon (2024) as the
    trend-day test. The combination is my own; numbers below are my own test.

  RULES (up trend -> long; down trend -> short, the mirror)
    R1 Session = 09:30-16:00 New York; holiday-shortened days: no trade.
    R2 Noise band as in NoiseBandMomentum: for each time of day, max(open,
       prior close) x (1 + average |close/open - 1| of the last 14 full
       sessions); the lower band mirrored.
    R3 Trend: at each 30-minute mark from 10:00, close > upper band -> UP,
       close < lower band -> DOWN (the latest one counts).
    R4 Entry, checked at each 15-minute boundary 10:00 .. 15:00: trend UP, the
       low of the last three 5-minute bars <= VWAP, and the close > VWAP
       -> LONG at market at the next open.
    R5 Attached stop 0.15 x the 14-session average day range. No target.
    R6 One trade a day. Flat at 15:55.

  MEASURED (this file in the mock, NQ 1-minute data 2017-01 .. 2024-03, 1 MNQ,
  $2.50 per round trip)
    677 trades | 28.8% winners | profit factor 1.56 | net +$13,427 |
    $154 per month (median $100) | 49 of 87 months positive | best month
    +$1,939, worst -$656 | max drawdown -$1,602 | longest losing streak 23 |
    3 best trades = 16% of the net | long +$8,354, short +$5,074.
    By year: 2017 +209, 2018 +1,308, 2019 -1,163, 2020 +1,426, 2021 +4,282,
    2022 +6,408, 2023 +745, 2024 (to March) +212.
    Chosen on 2017-2021 ($101/month), checked on 2022-2024 ($273/month).
    60 settings tested (band 0.8-1.3, stop 0.05-0.30, 5- or 15-minute block,
    1-2 trades a day): 100% profitable 2017-2021, 92% profitable 2022-2024.
    The 15-minute block is clearly better than 5 minutes; that choice is the
    one most exposed to over-fitting. 2019 lost $1,163: expect a year like it.
    NOT TESTED: anything after 2024-03-07.

  ORDER LAYER (identical in the three scripts of this suite)
    O1  One trade at a time, one order: quantity N, MaximumPositionAllowed = N.
    O2  The entry carries its own ATTACHED stop (Stop1Offset = a distance from
        the actual fill, so slippage never changes the risk). No target.
    O3  Entry on the first live call after the signal bar closes, at most 120 s
        late (input). Every engine signal is logged "TRADED" or
        "NOT traded -- <reason>".
    O4  Size: fixed contracts, or risk % of the ACCOUNT SIZE INPUT (never the
        broker balance, lessons #5) / the stop distance, capped by Max
        contracts; optional max $ risk per trade (reduces, then skips).
    O5  Risk halts, each OFF at 0 (#36): daily loss incl. the open trade
        (flattens), daily profit lock, losing trades in a row, trades per day.
        Reset only on a new trading day (#43).
    O6  Flat time 15:55 New York: resting entries cancelled, position closed
        with FlattenAndCancelAllOrders(); re-issued after 30 s, MANUAL ACTION
        line after 10 tries (#69). CME holiday / early-close sessions: no
        entries, flat the same distance before the halt (#66).
    O7  Reconciliation on every call from ONE position read (#17): closed
        trades classified from the CumulativeProfitLoss / TotalTrades delta
        (#34) before the daily rollover (#35); FILL VERIFY once per fill
        (#60); every contract covered by a working stop or flatten after 5 s
        (#68); orphan and inherited positions (#38, #70); working orders
        while flat are cancelled (#41).
    O8  Full recalculation: trade state survives, the engine is rebuilt, the
        first live call re-adopts or discards (#37, #38, #63).
    O9  Trade-behaviour settings are written on every call (#15, #16).
    O10 Live orders need 'Allow LIVE orders' = Yes. CHART CHECK (New York time
        zone, 5-minute bars, MNQ/NQ symbol, enough sessions) and the feed
        checks (connected; not delayed for live, #65) block entries.

  THREE BUILD STEPS IN ONE FILE
    Step 1 (engine): 'Orders' = Signals only -> signals, lines and the TALLY,
    no orders. Step 2 (orders) and Step 3 (hardening): 'Orders' = Send orders.
