/*============================================================================
  DailyWashoutRebound_NQ_MNQ  v1  --  engine + orders + hardening
  (The two lines above stay at the very top of the file.)

  WHAT IT DOES
    Cross-day mean reversion, long only. After a short, sharp sell-off in the
    DAILY closes the index tends to bounce during the next day session. The
    study measures the sell-off with a 2-period RSI of the last day-session
    closes; below 30 it buys the 09:30 open and holds to 15:55 with a wide
    volatility stop. Nothing here looks at intraday momentum: it buys
    weakness, on about one day in four.

  SOURCE OF THE IDEA
    Kaufman, Trading Systems and Methods 5th ed. (RSI and short-term
    overbought / oversold; patterns after a down close); Chan, Quantitative
    Trading / Algorithmic Trading (mean reversion of index returns at the
    daily horizon); the 2-period RSI is Connors' form of it. Checked here
    against five other definitions of "washed out" (close in the low fifth
    of the day's range, 2 and 3 down closes, a new 5- or 10-day low close,
    5-day return below -1 sd): every one was positive in both test periods.
    The mirror (sell after a run-up) was NOT: it is an input and is off.

  RULES
    R1 Session = 09:30-16:00 New York. Day-session close = the last trade
       before 16:00. RSI = Wilder RSI, length 2, of the returns between
       day-session closes (holiday-shortened sessions count; a session with
       under 35 percent of its bars does not). Needs 10 closes first.
    R2 avg range = mean high-low of the last 14 full day sessions.
    R3 On the 5-minute bar that ENDS at 09:30: RSI < 30 -> buy at market at
       the open. (The chart must hold the 09:25 bar: load the overnight
       session.) Holiday-shortened days: no trade.
    R4 Attached stop 0.75 x avg range below the fill. No target.
    R5 One trade a day. Flat at 15:55.

  MEASURED (this file in the mock, NQ 1-minute data 2017-01 .. 2024-03, 1 MNQ,
  $2.50 per round trip)
    465 trades | 54.8% winners | profit factor 1.38 | net +$15,706 |
    $181 per month (median $90) | 55 of 87 months positive | best month
    +$1,968, worst -$2,700 | max drawdown -$3,546 | longest losing streak 7 |
    3 best trades = 25% of the net | average win $223, average loss -$196.
    By year: 2017 +504, 2018 -803, 2019 +712, 2020 +5,292, 2021 +3,084,
    2022 +2,941, 2023 +3,116, 2024 (to March) +861.
    2017-2021: PF 1.49, $146/month. 2022-2024: PF 1.30, $264/month.
    At $5.00 per round trip: $167/month.
    Positive in both periods for RSI thresholds 20-40 and stops 0.5-3.0 x
    range. NOT for thresholds 10-15: the deepest washouts are not the best
    ones. Entering at 09:35 instead of the open gave up about a third of the
    edge. On ES the same rule is positive but weak (PF 1.09 / 1.14).
    THE RISK IS THE STOP, NOT THE AVERAGE LOSS: a full stop is 0.75 x the
    average day range (about $560 per MNQ if that range is 375 points). The
    worst month (-$2,700) is what this script looks like when the market
    keeps falling. A tight daily-loss halt makes it worse, not better.
    NOT TESTED: anything after 2024-03-07.

  ORDER LAYER (the layer of the MNQ momentum suite, unchanged; lessons #1-#90)
    O1  One trade at a time, one order: quantity N, MaximumPositionAllowed = N.
    O2  The entry is a MARKET order that carries its own ATTACHED stop
        (Stop1Offset = a distance from the actual fill, so slippage never
        changes the risk). No target, no stop modification: targets,
        breakeven moves and trailing stops were tested and did not help.
    O3  Entry on the first live call after the signal bar closes, at most 120 s
        late (input). Every engine signal is logged "TRADED" or
        "NOT traded -- <reason>".
    O4  Size: fixed contracts, or risk % of the ACCOUNT SIZE INPUT (never the
        broker balance) / the stop distance, capped by Max contracts; optional
        max $ risk per trade (reduces, then skips).
    O5  Risk halts, each OFF at 0: daily loss incl. the open trade (flattens),
        daily profit lock, losing trades in a row, trades per day. Reset only
        on a new trading day.
    O6  Flat time 15:55 New York: position closed with
        FlattenAndCancelAllOrders(); re-issued after 30 s, MANUAL ACTION line
        after 10 tries. CME holiday / early-close sessions: no entries, flat
        the same distance before the halt.
    O7  Reconciliation on every call from ONE position read: closed trades
        classified from the CumulativeProfitLoss / TotalTrades delta; FILL
        VERIFY once per fill; every contract covered by a working stop or
        flatten after 5 s; orphan and inherited positions; working orders
        while flat are cancelled.
    O8  Full recalculation: trade state survives, the engine is rebuilt, the
        first live call re-adopts or discards. Signals are keyed by time.
    O9  Trade-behaviour settings are written on every call.
    O10 Live orders need 'Allow LIVE orders' = Yes. CHART CHECK (New York time
        zone, 5-minute bars, MNQ/NQ symbol, enough sessions) and the feed
        checks (connected; not delayed for live) block entries.
    ONE STRATEGY PER TRADE ACCOUNT: this study flattens a position it did not
    open. Do not run it on the same account as another study of this
    repository (including MomentumSuite) -- give each its own Sim account.

  THREE BUILD STEPS IN ONE FILE
    Step 1 (engine): 'Orders' = Signals only -> signals, lines and the TALLY,
    no orders. Step 2 (orders) and Step 3 (hardening): 'Orders' = Send orders.
