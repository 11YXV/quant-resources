  ValueAreaReentry_NQ_MNQ  v1  --  engine + orders + hardening
  (The two lines above stay at the very top of the file.)

  WHAT IT DOES
    Auction-market mean reversion (the "80 percent rule" of Market Profile).
    The prior day session's VALUE AREA is the price band that held 70% of
    its volume. When today opens OUTSIDE that band and a 30-minute close
    comes back INSIDE it, the open has been rejected: the study trades
    towards the other side of value (short if the open was above, long if
    below) and holds to 15:55. The opposite bet to an opening-drive breakout.

  SOURCE OF THE IDEA
    Steidlmayer / Dalton, Market Profile and auction-market theory: value
    area, point of control, and the rule that a market opening outside value
    and re-entering it tends to cross it. Kaufman, Trading Systems and
    Methods 5th ed. (price distribution, Market Profile). Tested here: one
    30-minute close inside is enough; a target at the far side or at the
    point of control was WORSE than holding to the close; fading the
    value-area edges on days that open inside value LOSES.

  RULES
    R1 Session = 09:30-16:00 New York. Volume profile of the prior FULL day
       session from this chart's 5-minute bars: each bar's volume is spread
       evenly over its high-low range in 1-point bins. Point of control =
       the bin with the most volume; the value area grows one bin at a time
       towards the larger neighbour until it holds 70% of the volume.
    R2 Today's open above the value-area high -> short side armed; below the
       value-area low -> long side armed; inside -> no trade today.
    R3 At 10:00, 10:30, ... 12:30: the first 30-minute close strictly inside
       the value area -> enter at market in the armed direction.
    R4 Attached stop 0.20 x avg range (mean high-low of the last 14 full
       sessions). No target. One trade a day. Flat at 15:55.
       Holiday-shortened days, and days after one: no trade.
