/*============================================================================
  EndOfDayFlow_NQ_MNQ  v1  --  engine + orders + hardening
  (The two lines above stay at the very top of the file.)

  WHAT IT DOES
    One trade in the last hour, only on days that have already moved at
    least 1% from yesterday's day-session close. At 15:00 New York it goes
    WITH that move and holds to 15:55. About one day in three.

  WHY IT SHOULD WORK (published mechanism, not proven here)
    Leveraged and inverse ETFs must rebalance at every close by an amount
    proportional to the day's return, in its direction: a 3x fund buys
    after an up day and sells after a down day. Option dealers who are short
    gamma hedge the same way. Both flows are mechanical, not informational,
    and they concentrate near the close. Nasdaq-100 has the largest
    leveraged-ETF complex relative to its futures market (TQQQ / SQQQ grew
    roughly tenfold 2017-2022), which fits this effect being NQ-specific
    in this data.
      * Baltussen, Da, Lammers, Martens (2021), "Hedging demand and market
        intraday momentum", Journal of Financial Economics 142.
      * Barbon, Beckmeyer, Buraschi, Moerke (2021-22), "Liquidity provision
        to leveraged ETFs and equity options rebalancing flows: evidence
        from end-of-day stock prices", SSRN 3925725 / SFI 22-40. They find
        the leveraged-ETF channel larger than the option channel, and later
        work finds the leveraged-ETF part has weakened over time.

  HOW IT DIFFERS FROM THE MOMENTUM SUITE
    The suite's legs decide from the size of the move against an intraday
    noise band, mostly in the morning, and hold whatever trend they find.
    This decides once, at 15:00, from a fixed percent move against the
    prior close, and holds 55 minutes. It does go WITH the day's move, so it
    is the same direction as the suite on many days: daily P&L correlation
    with the suite 0.26 (with the three reversion scripts 0.13).

  RULES
    R1 Session = 09:30-16:00 New York. Prior close = last trade of the prior
       day session (one with at least 35% of its bars).
    R2 At the close of the 14:55-15:00 bar: move = close / prior close - 1.
       |move| >= 1.0% -> buy (move up) or sell (move down) at market.
       No trade if that bar is missing, on CME holiday-shortened days, or
       before 14 full sessions of history.
    R3 Attached stop 0.40 x avg range (mean high-low of the last 14 full
       sessions). No target. One trade a day. Flat at 15:55.

  MEASURED (this file in the mock, NQ 1-minute data 2017-01 .. 2024-03, 1 MNQ,
  $2.50 per round trip)

    2017-2021: PF 1.54, $73/month. 2022-2024: PF 2.31, $313/month.

    READ BEFORE TRUSTING IT
      * Half of all profit is from 2022. Quiet years (2017, 2019, 2021) made
        about nothing: it trades rarely when days do not move 1%.
      * In 2017-2021 the result depends on the exit minute: leaving at 15:50
        or 15:59 instead of 15:55 cut that period from PF 1.54 to 1.25 (and
        to 1.0 with a 0.25 stop). 2022-2024 held up at every exit minute
        (PF 2.1-2.3).
      * Measured against the average range (instead of percent) the same
        idea was flat in 2017-2021. On ES it lost in 2017-2021 and won in
        2022-2024; on YM it did nothing. Treat it as an NQ result that
        became strong after 2021, with a plausible structural reason.
      * A daily-loss halt tighter than one full stop turns it negative
        (mock: -$2,660 with 2 contracts and a $50 halt). Leave it off or set
        it wider than one stop.
      * About 85 ideas have now been tried across all rounds.
    Positive in both periods in 58 of 64 settings (decision 14:30-15:15,
    min move 0.75-1.5%, stop 0.15 / 0.25 / 0.4 / none).
    NOT TESTED: anything after 2024-03-07.

  ORDER LAYER (the layer of the MNQ momentum suite, unchanged; lessons #1-#90)
    O1  One trade at a time, one order: quantity N, MaximumPositionAllowed = N.
    O2  The entry is a MARKET order that carries its own ATTACHED stop
        (Stop1Offset = a distance from the actual fill, so slippage never
        changes the risk). No target, no stop modification: targets,
        breakeven moves and trailing stops were tested and did not help.
    O3  Entry on the first live call after the signal bar closes, at most 120 s
        late (input). Every engine signal is logged "TRADED" or
        "NOT traded -- <reason>".
    O4  Size: fixed contracts, or risk % of the ACCOUNT SIZE INPUT (never the
        broker balance) / the stop distance, capped by Max contracts; optional
        max $ risk per trade (reduces, then skips).
    O5  Risk halts, each OFF at 0: daily loss incl. the open trade (flattens),
        daily profit lock, losing trades in a row, trades per day. Reset only
        on a new trading day.
    O6  Flat time 15:55 New York: position closed with
        FlattenAndCancelAllOrders(); re-issued after 30 s, MANUAL ACTION line
        after 10 tries. CME holiday / early-close sessions: no entries, flat
        the same distance before the halt.
    O7  Reconciliation on every call from ONE position read: closed trades
        classified from the CumulativeProfitLoss / TotalTrades delta; FILL
        VERIFY once per fill; every contract covered by a working stop or
        flatten after 5 s; orphan and inherited positions; working orders
        while flat are cancelled.
    O8  Full recalculation: trade state survives, the engine is rebuilt, the
        first live call re-adopts or discards. Signals are keyed by time.
    O9  Trade-behaviour settings are written on every call.
    O10 Live orders need 'Allow LIVE orders' = Yes. CHART CHECK (New York time
        zone, 5-minute bars, MNQ/NQ symbol, enough sessions) and the feed
        checks (connected; not delayed for live) block entries.
    ONE STRATEGY PER TRADE ACCOUNT: this study flattens a position it did not
    open. Do not run it on the same account as another study of this
    repository (including MomentumSuite) -- give each its own Sim account.

  THREE BUILD STEPS IN ONE FILE
    Step 1 (engine): 'Orders' = Signals only -> signals, lines and the TALLY,
    no orders. Step 2 (orders) and Step 3 (hardening): 'Orders' = Send orders.
