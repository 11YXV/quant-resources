  CompressionExpansionBreakout_NQ_MNQ  v1  --  engine + orders + hardening
  (The two lines above stay at the very top of the file.)

  WHAT IT DOES
    Volatility cycle. Quiet days are followed by active ones. The study
    trades ONLY on the day after a narrow-range day (yesterday's day-session
    range was the smallest of the last 4 full sessions, "NR4"). On such a
    day the first 5-minute close outside the 30-minute opening range is
    followed in its direction, with a volatility stop, to 15:55. On all
    other days (about three in four) it does nothing.

  SOURCE OF THE IDEA
    Crabel's narrow-range patterns (NR4 / NR7) as described in Kaufman,
    Trading Systems and Methods 5th ed. (opening range breakout; volatility);
    volatility clustering in Tsay, Analysis of Financial Time Series.
    Tested here: the same breakout taken EVERY day is close to worthless
    (PF 1.03 in 2017-2021); the narrow-range filter is what carries it.
    It is a breakout, so it is the closest of the three new scripts to the
    momentum suite, but the trigger is yesterday's compression, not today's
    move size or the overnight gap (daily correlation with the suite 0.25).

  RULES
    R1 Session = 09:30-16:00 New York. Range of a session = high - low.
       Yesterday must be a full session and its range must be the smallest
       of the last 4 full sessions.
    R2 Opening range = 09:30-10:00 (six 5-minute bars, none missing).
    R3 From 10:05 to 13:00: the first 5-minute bar that CLOSES above the
       opening-range high -> long at market; below its low -> short.
    R4 Attached stop 0.25 x avg range (mean high-low of the last 14 full
       sessions). No target. One trade a day. Flat at 15:55.
       Holiday-shortened days: no trade.
